# Wylie Grizzlies Baseball Walk-Up Music App ⚾🔴

A custom walk-up music soundboard tailored for the **Wylie Grizzlies (8U Fall 2026)** team, sporting the team's official **Crimson Red, Carbon Black, and Chalk White** colors, optimized for full-screen use on an **iPhone 16 Pro Max**.

---

## 🎨 Authentic Team Colors & Logo

Updated directly to match the team's uniforms:
- **Primary:** Wylie Crimson Red (`#D61827` / `#FF2E3E`)
- **Secondary:** Dugout Carbon Black (`#08080B` / `#15171E`)
- **Accent:** Chalk White (`#FFFFFF`) & Heather Grey (`#9CA3AF`)
- **Jersey Badges:** Red jersey badge with crisp white athletic numbers (#6, #7, #12, #99, etc.), directly matching the red jerseys and white typography from the team photo.
- **Team Logo:** Red, black, and white roaring Grizzly Bear shield with crossed bats and baseball stitches.

---

## 📱 How to Run Full-Screen on iPhone 16 Pro Max

1. Connect your iPhone to the same Wi-Fi network as this Mac.
2. Open Safari on your iPhone and go to:
   ```
   http://192.168.4.91:8844
   ```
   *(Or on your Mac at `http://localhost:8844`)*
3. **Make it 100% Full-Screen (No Safari bars):**
   - Tap the **Share** button in Safari (the square with an arrow pointing up).
   - Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   - Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
   - Open the new **"Grizzlies"** icon on your home screen — it will launch in standalone native full-screen mode!

- **#14 Jordan Taylor** — *Walk-Up Track*

- **#20 Maddox Hurst** — *Walk-Up Track*
- **#22 West Shields** — *Walk-Up Track*
- **#56 Jonah Ercan** — *I’m Shipping Up to Boston (Dropkick Murphys)*
- **#99 Garrett Dowdy** — *Jesus Is Alive*

Each button features:
- Large high-contrast athletic jersey number (#6, #12, #99, etc.) on a red jersey badge
- Player full name and walk-up track title
- Animated red audio equalizer bars and glowing stadium red border when active
- Elapsed progress bar across the bottom of the card

### 2. Stadium Hype Sounds
Instant rally sound effects for big innings:
- 📣 **"GET ON YOUR FEET"** (Stadium rally horn & beat)
- 🐻 **"GRIZZLIES FANS"** (Crowd chants & organ hype)

### 3. Fade Out Slider & Quick Actions
- **FADE OUT Button:** Single tap triggers a smooth exponential studio fade-out over your configured duration.
- **Fade Duration Slider:** Adjust fade length from **0.5s to 5.0s** (default 2.0s).
- **Preset Chips:** Quick-tap chips (`1.0s`, `2.0s`, `3.0s`, `4.0s`) to switch fade speeds instantly.
- **CUT / STOP Button:** Instant kill switch when the batter steps into the box.
- **Master Volume Slider:** Touch-friendly fader for manual thumb fading from 0% to 100%.

### 4. Dugout Lineup & On-Deck Management
- **Lineup Reorder:** Tap the **Lineup** button in the header to reorder batters to match today's game lineup.
- **On-Deck Banner:** When a song finishes or fades, the app automatically flags the next batter in the batting order with a **"PLAY NEXT →"** button.
- **Search Bar:** Quick search by jersey number (e.g. `12`) or player name.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts (Desktop/iPad):** `Space` to instant cut/stop, `F` to fade out.
