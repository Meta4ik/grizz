Full-screen tips for iPhone 16 Pro Max

📱 True Full-Screen Mode

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner.
  5. Launch from your Home Screen — it will run 100% full screen with zero browser address bars!

🎚️ Fade Out & Controls

  • FADE OUT Button: Tapping smoothly lowers volume to zero over your chosen fade time.
  • Fade Duration Slider: Drag between 0.5s and 5.0s, or tap the quick 1s, 2s, 3s, 4s chips.
  • CUT / STOP Button: Instant silence when the batter steps into the batter's box.
  • Lineup Button: Arrange today's batting order to see who is On Deck next!
